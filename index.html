ATOMIX ORIGINAL

Edge Of The Universe

An adventure beyond reality.

Edge Of The Universe is a science-fiction adventure game developed by AtomiX Corp.

After being pulled into an unfamiliar alien reality, the player must explore mysterious worlds and discover a way back home.

Across a universe filled with strange environments and unknown challenges, the journey is centered around discovering powerful crystals and finding a way to escape.

Explore the worlds. Collect the crystals. Find your way home.
